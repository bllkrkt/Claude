## Long description — English
Efsane Avcısı is a one-handed, fully offline 3D action RPG. Tap, strike, chain combos — then unlock auto attack and turn your hero into a legend.

⚔️ 3 CLASSES, 12 SKILLS
Play as a Warrior, Archer or Mage, each with a unique attack style and four powerful skills.

🗺️ 8 REGIONS, HUNDREDS OF FLOORS
Journey from the Forest to the Void. Every 10th floor brings a timed boss fight. The final region never ends.

🔮 PATH OF FATE
After every floor, choose one of three powers. Powers stack, so every run becomes a different adventure.

🛡️ SMART ENEMIES
Shielded, swarming, healing and exploding monsters. Parry boss special attacks at the right moment.

🎒 DEEP LOOT
4 rarities, 5 gear sets, legendary items with unique powers, runes and a blacksmith up to +15. Equip your best gear and upgrade everything in one tap.

🏰 ADVENTURE MODES
Boss Arena, dungeons, a duel league, the Endless Tower and daily quests.

🐾 COMPANIONS AND MASTERY
Companions that fight and evolve with you, a mastery tree, rebirth and the permanent Soul Tree.

📶 OFFLINE, NO ADS
The game never connects to the internet and collects no personal data. Play anywhere.
